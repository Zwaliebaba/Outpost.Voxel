# ADR-025 — The design generator, and how a mount's name carries its size

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-29 · **Lands with:** phase 1 of [`Design/MvpPlan.md`](../MvpPlan.md), its tasks 2 to 4 · **Amends:** [ADR-023](ADR-023-tests-and-tools-folders.md), whose `Tools/` holds a second tool with no C++; and [`NeuronVoxelFormat.md`](../Archive/NeuronVoxelFormat.md) §5, whose marker names this gives a convention for mounts, which the game concept left to G-M1 (its §5.2)

## Context

The owner chose to have the MVP's designs generated by the agent's script rather than authored in MagicaVoxel (the plan's §2.3). The MVP needs:
- five hulls with mounts: the miner, gunship, lancer and cruiser, and the skirmish's station core;
- the modules those mounts hold;
- three asteroids of distinct shapes.

NVF's N1 keeps a design's voxels in MagicaVoxel's files, from which `NvfImport` makes the `.nvf` the game reads. A generator that wrote `.nvf` directly would be a second owner of those voxels. Writing `.vox` keeps one owner: a generated design can be opened in MagicaVoxel, edited, and replaced by a hand-made one with the same mounts, with no change to anything downstream.

The concept gives each mount a type and a size class, and leaves to G-M1 how a marker's name carries the size (its §5.2). NVF §5 names a marker `<part>@<hardpoint>`, and §4.1 spells a hardpoint name as segments of `[a-z0-9_]`, the first of them its type.

## Decision

**The tool.** `Tools/DesignGenerator/` is Python:
- `DesignGenerator.py` describes the models;
- `VoxFile.py` writes them as `.vox` files.

It sits beside the Blender extension in `Tools/`, as ADR-023 allows for a tool with no C++. It needs no third-party package and no Blender. Run from the repository root, it rewrites `GameData`'s generated `.vox` files and nothing else. With `--check` it writes nothing, and exits 1 if any file differs.

**The files are committed, and so are their `.nvf`.** `NvfImport` makes each `.nvf` from its `.vox`, and CI's model check holds the two together, as it does for the three assets (NVF §9). So each generated model is held to three things:
- the generator's suite holds the `.vox` to the generator's output, byte for byte;
- the model check holds the `.nvf` to the `.vox`;
- `GameCoreTests` holds the design to the concept's validation (ADR-024).

**A mount's name is `<type>.<size>.<label>`.** Its marker is `hull@<type>.<size>.<label>`, which is within NVF §5's grammar.
- The **type** is the hardpoint type of the module it holds: `command`, `reactor`, `engine`, `weapon`, `mining`, `cargo`, `sensor`, `shipyard`, `lab` or `refinery`.
- The **size** is `s` or `l`.
- The **label** tells mounts of one type and size apart, such as `port`, `dorsal_fore` or `main`.

A size names the box the mount's largest module fills, in the mount's frame, +Z the way it faces:
- `s` is 3 × 3 × 5;
- `l` is 5 × 5 × 9.

Both are odd on every axis, so the box's center is a voxel's center, as NVF §5 requires of a marker. A name with more or fewer segments, another size or an unknown type is refused by `GameCore` as `BadMountName`.

**The marker is the box.** A mount's marker is its box, drawn in magenta (palette entry 15, which no hull uses) and turned by the mount's facing. So MagicaVoxel shows exactly the cells the concept's §5.5 keeps clear. The importer keeps the marker's center and turn and drops its voxels (NVF §6.2).

**Hulls.** Each hull is a single part named `hull`, whose least cell is the origin. A mount inside the hull, such as the command module's, has its box carved out of the hull. The generator refuses, before writing, any hull that breaks the concept's rules:
- two command mounts, or none;
- a box that holds a hull voxel, overlaps another box, or shares no face with the hull;
- a blocked line, which the concept does not refuse (G38) but no generated design should have;
- a hull that is not one piece.

**Modules.** A module is a single unnamed part, at most its size's box. Its model faces +Z, and its center voxel, ⌊size / 2⌋ on each axis, is the mount's center when the game places it.

**Palettes.** For every hull:
- entries 1 and 2 are light armor, and 3 and 4 heavy, the pairing ADR-024's catalogue reads (G13);
- entry 5 is trim and entry 6 lights, both light armor;
- entry 15 is the markers' magenta;
- entry 16 is the side's color (G24): the renderer gives each side its own.

A module's palette is its own, and its entry 16 is the side's color too.

**Asteroids** are ellipsoids with bumps and craters placed by a Python twin of `NeuronCore`'s `PcgHash`, so the noise is integer arithmetic and the same on every machine. Each keeps its largest face-connected piece.

**CI.** The Linux job runs the generator's suite on the system Python, beside the Blender extension's (`.github/workflows/build.yml`).

## Figures

The generator writes 21 files, 252,610 bytes in all. It writes the same bytes on every run, and the suite checks that. Its 18 tests pass on Python 3.11, and so do the Blender extension's 59, 7 of them skipped without Blender.

| Hull | Voxels | Heavy | Extent, x × y × z | Mounts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Miner | 1,210 | 130 | 9 × 9 × 17 | command, reactor, cargo hold, mining laser, two engines, sensor |
| Gunship | 1,155 | 498 | 13 × 5 × 27 | command, reactor, two engines, two weapons on its back, sensor |
| Lancer | 1,119 | 395 | 7 × 8 × 36 | command, reactor, two engines, a weapon on each flank, sensor |
| Cruiser | 9,017 | 5,707 | 17 × 13 × 61 | command, large reactor, four engines, two weapons on its back and one on each beam, sensor |
| StationCore | 28,955 | 5,264 | 41 × 25 × 41 | command, large reactor, large shipyard, lab, refinery and sensor array, a weapon on each edge of its roof |

- **Modules.** Thirteen, one for each kind and size that a design fits. They range from the sensor's 13 voxels to the large reactor's, lab's and refinery's 225.
- **Asteroids.** A has 2,027 voxels in 17 × 18 × 19, B 4,676 in 25 × 24 × 21, and C 1,330 in 16 × 14 × 24.

The voxel counts differ from the plan's "about" figures (§8.1) where ADR-024's acceleration targets needed them to. Phase 1's pull request updates that table.

## What this forecloses

- A generated `.nvf` with no `.vox` behind it, or a generated file edited by hand. Change the generator and run it.
- A mount name that does not say its size, and a size other than the two boxes.
- A marker whose voxels mean anything: only its center and turn are kept.
